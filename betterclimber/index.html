Camera not working

Check that BetterClimber has camera permission enabled in the device settings. The app only needs camera access while actively recording a climb.

FAQ

Short answers to the questions users actually ask.

The quick policy answers are still here, but this section now also covers product support, metrics, backups, and Pro access.

How does BetterClimber analyze my climbing?

BetterClimber uses on-device AI pose detection to track 33 body landmarks in real time. All processing happens on your phone or tablet, not on external servers.

What do the efficiency metrics mean?

  • COM Stability: how steady your center of mass stays during moves.
  • Body Tension: how well you maintain body control on the wall.
  • Hip Proximity: how close your hips stay to the wall.
  • Movement Smoothness: how fluid and controlled your movement is.

Can I analyze videos I already recorded?

Yes. BetterClimber Pro can import existing climbing videos from your gallery, then run local pose analysis, skeleton overlay, and movement heatmaps on-device.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your device unless you explicitly share a video or export a backup file.

Does the app upload my videos or record sound?

No. Session recordings and imported videos are processed locally, and BetterClimber does not record audio.

How do I restore my data on a new device?

Use Backup & Restore in Settings. Your backup transfers session metadata, preferences, and other app data. Video files are not included because they are too large.

The pose detection looks off. What should I check?

  • Use good lighting so your body is clearly visible.
  • Keep your full body inside the camera frame.
  • Stabilize the phone with a tripod or solid support.
  • Wear clothing that contrasts with the wall background.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Delete sessions individually from History, clear app data in device settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local BetterClimber data.

Full Privacy Policy

The complete policy text remains on-page for reviewers and users.

Nothing important is hidden behind another route. The summary above is for scannability; the full policy below remains explicit and reviewer-accessible.

Privacy first: all pose analysis happens entirely on your device. Videos and climbing data are never uploaded to external servers by BetterClimber.

Data Collection

BetterClimber collects and stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Profile Information: Your chosen display name (optional)
  • Session Recordings: Videos and pose data from your climbing sessions
  • Imported Videos: When you import videos for analysis, pose data is extracted and stored locally
  • App Preferences: Theme settings, goals, and achievements
  • Usage Statistics: Session counts and personal bests for tracking your progress

Camera Access

The app requires camera access to record your climbing sessions and perform real-time pose analysis. This permission is used only when you actively record a session. No audio is recorded — the app only captures video.

Photo Library Access

With the Pro video import feature, the app may request access to your photo library to let you select existing climbing videos for analysis. Videos are processed locally and never uploaded.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. Data includes:

  • Video files stored in app-private storage
  • Pose landmark data stored as JSON files
  • Settings and preferences stored in encrypted app storage

Data Sharing

BetterClimber does not share your data with any third parties. The only way data leaves your device is:

  • When you explicitly choose to share a video using the share function
  • When you export a backup file to transfer to another device

In-App Purchases

BetterClimber offers optional Pro features through in-app purchases (subscription or lifetime purchase). Purchase transactions are handled entirely by the platform's app store (Google Play or Apple App Store) and are subject to their respective privacy policies. We do not have access to your payment information.

Analytics & Tracking

BetterClimber does not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. We do not collect any usage analytics or behavioral data.

Children's Privacy

BetterClimber is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual sessions from the History page
  • Clearing app data through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all local data)
