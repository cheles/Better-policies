Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 26, 2026

BetterDog ("the App") is developed by Claudiu Cheles ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

BetterDog is designed to be privacy-first. All dog profiles, training sessions, vet records, photos, and app settings are stored entirely on your device.

  • Dog profiles & training data: Stored locally on your device only. Never sent to our servers.
  • Vet records & feeding schedules: Stored locally. Never transmitted off your device.
  • Photos: Progress photos are stored on your device. Only uploaded if you opt into cloud backup.

2. Optional Cloud Backup

If you choose to sign in and enable cloud backup, your data is:

  • Encrypted on your device before upload using AES encryption with a key stored in your device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Stored in Google Firebase/Firestore under your personal account scope.
  • Only accessible by you — we cannot read your encrypted data.

Cloud backup is entirely optional. The app is fully functional without it.

3. Sign-In & Authentication

BetterDog offers three sign-in methods:

  • Google Sign-In: Available on Android and iOS.
  • Apple Sign-In: Available on iOS. Uses a secure nonce-based authentication flow with Firebase.
  • Email/Password: Available on all platforms. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password.

Your display name and email address are used solely to authenticate your account for cloud backup. This information is handled entirely within Google Firebase — we do not store, transmit, or share it on any other server or with any third party.

4. Notifications

The app may request permission to send local push notifications for training reminders, feeding schedules, and vet appointment alerts. These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external server.

4.5. Permissions Overview

BetterDog requests only the permissions needed for the features you choose to use:

  • Notifications: Used to deliver local reminders for feeding schedules, medication, training, and vet care. These reminders are created on your device.
  • Camera: Used when you choose to take a profile photo or progress photo for your dog.
  • Photo Library: Used when you choose an existing photo from your device for your dog's profile or progress tracking.
  • Internet Connection: Required only for optional account sign-in, cloud backup, restore, and other cloud-based features. BetterDog remains usable offline for local-only features.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Used to provide tactile clicker feedback during training sessions.
  • Audio Playback Control: Used to play clicker sounds and training sounds reliably, including when your device is in silent mode where supported.

Permissions are requested in context when you use the related feature, not on first launch. If you deny a permission, BetterDog will continue to work, but the related feature may be unavailable until you enable that permission in your device settings.

5. Photo Library

The app accesses your photo library only when you choose to add a profile picture for your dog or select a progress photo. If you take a new photo, BetterDog uses your device camera only for that action. Photos are stored locally and only uploaded if cloud backup is enabled.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Used only if you opt into cloud backup. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In: Used for authentication on Android and iOS. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign-In: Used for authentication on iOS. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase: Optional donations processed entirely through the platform's native billing system. Subject to Google's / Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or crash reporting services.

7. Terms of Use

Use of BetterDog is subject to the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

8. Account & Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data at all times.

Delete Your Account & Data from the App

Open the app and follow these steps:

Settings → Reset All Data → ✅ "+ Delete cloud backups & account data" → Confirm

This permanently deletes your Firebase account, all cloud backups (encrypted dog profiles, training data, vet records, photos, settings), and any data stored in Firestore under your account. Local data on your device is also cleared.

Alternative — by email: Send a request to claudiu@betterclimber.app with the subject "BetterDog Account Deletion Request" and the email address used for sign-in. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What gets deleted:

  • Your Firebase authentication account
  • All cloud backup data (encrypted dog profiles, training sessions, vet records, photos, settings)
  • Any data stored in Google Firestore under your account
  • All local dog profiles, training history, photos, and settings on the device

What is NOT affected:

  • Google Play / App Store purchase records (managed by the platform)

9. Children's Privacy

BetterDog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
