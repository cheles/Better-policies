Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 21, 2026

BetterLife ("the App") is developed by Claudiu Cheles ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

BetterLife is designed to be privacy-first. All journal entries, voice recordings, transcriptions, photos, and AI analysis are processed and stored entirely on your device.

  • Journal entries & photos: Stored locally on your device only. Never sent to our servers.
  • Voice recordings: Processed by on-device speech recognition. Audio is never transmitted off your device.
  • AI classification & sentiment: Performed by an on-device ONNX model. No data is sent to any external API.
  • Health & wellness information: Journal entries you write or dictate may contain health-related content (e.g. exercise, sleep, diet, stress, medical visits, mental well-being). The app's on-device AI automatically categorizes entries into 8 life areas, one of which is Health, and performs sentiment analysis to track trends over time. All health data is processed and stored entirely on your device. It is never sent to our servers or any third party. If you optionally enable cloud backup, this data is encrypted on your device before upload and only you can decrypt it.

2. Optional Cloud Backup

If you choose to sign in and enable cloud backup (a PRO feature), your data is:

  • Encrypted on your device before upload using AES-equivalent encryption derived from your account credentials.
  • Stored in Google Firebase/Firestore under your personal account scope.
  • Only accessible by you — we cannot read your encrypted data.

Cloud backup is entirely optional. The app is fully functional without it.

3. Sign-In & Authentication

BetterLife offers three sign-in methods:

  • Google Sign-In: Available on Android and iOS.
  • Apple Sign-In: Available on iOS. Uses a secure nonce-based authentication flow with Firebase.
  • Email/Password: Available on all platforms. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password.

Your display name and email address are used solely to authenticate your account for cloud backup. This information is handled entirely within Google Firebase — we do not store, transmit, or share it on any other server or with any third party.

4. Notifications

The app may request permission to send local push notifications for daily journaling reminders. These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external server.

5. Permissions We Request

BetterLife only requests permissions that are necessary for its core features. Below is a complete list for each platform.

iOS Permissions

  • Microphone — Used for voice journaling. On-device speech recognition transcribes your words; audio never leaves your device.
  • Speech Recognition — Transcribes your spoken journal entries locally on your device. No audio or text is sent to external servers.
  • Photo Library — Accessed only when you choose to attach a Day Snap photo to a journal entry.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — Used by the App Lock feature (PRO) to secure access to your journal. Biometric data is managed entirely by iOS and never accessed by the app.
  • Notifications — Used for daily journaling reminders. Scheduled locally on your device with no external server involved.
  • Sign in with Apple — Optional authentication method for cloud backup. Handled via Apple's secure authentication framework.
  • In-App Purchases — Used to offer the PRO upgrade through Apple's App Store billing.

Android Permissions

  • Microphone — When you record a voice journal entry, the app uses on-device speech recognition to turn your words into text. Audio never leaves your device.
  • Notifications — Sends your daily journaling reminders. These are scheduled entirely on your device — no external server is involved.
  • Full network access — Used to sign in to your account, sync encrypted backups to the cloud, and process PRO purchases. The app works fully offline — internet is only used when you choose to back up or sign in.
  • View network connections — Checks whether you are online or offline so the app can switch smoothly between local-only mode and cloud sync.
  • Change your audio settings — When you record a voice entry, the app temporarily pauses other audio (like music) so speech recognition can hear you clearly. It resumes everything when you stop recording.
  • Run at startup — Restarts your daily journaling reminders after your phone reboots. Without this, reminders would disappear every time you restart your device.
  • Control vibration — Provides subtle haptic feedback when you tap buttons and when notification reminders fire.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Used only if you opt into cloud backup. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In: Used for authentication on Android and iOS. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign-In: Used for authentication on iOS. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase: PRO purchase processed entirely through the platform's native billing system. Subject to Google's / Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or crash reporting services.

7. Terms of Use

Use of BetterLife is subject to the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

8. Account & Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data at all times.

Delete Your Account & Data from the App

Open the app and follow these steps:

Settings → Delete Account → Confirm

This permanently deletes your Firebase account, all cloud backups (encrypted journal entries, photos, settings), and any data stored in Firestore under your account. Local data on your device is also cleared. This action cannot be undone.

Alternative — by email: Send a request to claudiu@betterclimber.app with the subject "BetterLife Account Deletion Request" and the email address used for Google Sign-In. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What gets deleted:

  • Your Firebase authentication account
  • All cloud backup data (encrypted journal entries, photos, settings)
  • Any data stored in Google Firestore under your account
  • All local journal entries, photos, and life scores on the device

What is NOT affected:

  • Google Play / App Store purchase records (managed by the platform)
